Block threshold

05 Providers

AI checks try providers top to bottom and fall through when one is missing, out of quota or fails. Local models are free but state their own confidence, so thresholds are rougher than with Clef and JEV, whose probabilities are calibrated. The stub names the provider that hid a tweet.
